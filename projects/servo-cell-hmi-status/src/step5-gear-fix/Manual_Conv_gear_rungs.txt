Manual_Conv - electronic gearing rungs
==========================================================================

Source of truth is TECE1250_GreenMachine_V7_7_GearFix.L5X.

V7_7 = V7_6 with one operand changed: axis 145's MAG used MAG_144 as its motion
control tag, the same tag as axis 144's instruction. No new tags, no new rungs.

Master is 141. Followers are 142-145. Ratio is Gear_Ratio_SP (currently 1.0).
Engage requires Mode_Manual and all five Enable_14x.

While GearingStatus is true, the manual jog/move rungs for 142-145 are blocked by
XIO(...GearingStatus) - jog the master, not a follower.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
