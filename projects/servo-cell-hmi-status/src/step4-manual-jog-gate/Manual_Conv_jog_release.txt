Manual_Conv jog-release rungs - neutral text listing
==========================================================================

Source of truth is TECE1250_GreenMachine_V7_6_JogGate.L5X.

V7_6 = V7_5 plus one contact on five rungs, and Stop_PB shipping as 0.
Cumulative - it contains the status pill, the manual faceplate groundwork
and the sequencer upgrade as well.

To fix by hand instead of importing: add XIC(Mode_Manual) to the front of
rungs 9, 19, 29, 39 and 49 of Manual_Conv.
